How we work

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Curious how it's built? Browse the code on GitHub ↗
Is the247 a company or a personal portfolio? +
Both, really — it's a small studio run by three co-founders, currently doubling as a live portfolio of everything shipped so far.
Do you build for outside clients? +
Yes — Sabaaneh, Shadi Zaqtan, and Rootsapiens are real client sites, live today, listed above with their own domains.
What does "AI-assisted development" actually mean here? +
Design and code are produced end-to-end with AI tooling doing the heavy lifting on scaffolding, migrations and boilerplate — a human still scopes, reviews, and ships every release.
What's the usual stack? +
React/Next.js or Vue on the front end, Firebase or PostgreSQL behind it, Python for automation, and the Gemini API where AI features are involved.
What do the status badges mean? +
Live is in production today, beta/wip is functional but still being hardened, and idea/concept is a mockup or early-stage build.
Who — or what — is LaRose? +
An AI Chief-of-Staff role on the team, not a person — it runs the weekly agenda and decision log under human approval, listed on the team page for transparency.
